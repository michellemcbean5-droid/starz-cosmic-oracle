# Privacy Policy — Starz Cosmic Oracle

**Last Updated: July 2025**

## Introduction

Starz Cosmic Oracle ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Birth Date and Time**: Used for birth chart calculations and personalized horoscopes
- **Birth Location**: Used for rising sign calculations
- **Name**: Optional, used for personalization
- **Dream Descriptions**: Optional, used for dream interpretation features
- **Zodiac Sign**: Used for horoscope readings

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, OS version, app version
- **Usage Data**: Feature usage, reading history (stored locally)
- **Crash Reports**: Anonymous crash data for app improvement

### Information from Third Parties
- **RevenueCat**: Purchase and subscription data (for IAP processing)
- **AdMob**: Advertising identifiers (for free tier ads only)
- **HuggingFace**: Dream/horoscope text sent for AI processing (not retained)

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Astrological Calculations**: Birth data is used to generate accurate birth charts, horoscopes, and planetary transits
2. **Personalization**: Name and sign preferences customize your experience
3. **AI Features**: Text inputs (dreams, questions) are sent to HuggingFace API for interpretation and immediately discarded
4. **Monetization**: RevenueCat processes subscription purchases
5. **Advertising**: AdMob serves ads to free tier users
6. **App Improvement**: Anonymous usage and crash data

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: All personal data (birth date, readings, history) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not upload your personal data to our servers
- **AI Processing**: Text sent to HuggingFace is processed in real-time and not retained
- **Subscription Data**: RevenueCat stores purchase receipts on their secure servers

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with:
- **RevenueCat**: For in-app purchase processing
- **AdMob**: For advertising (free tier only)
- **HuggingFace**: For AI feature processing (text only, not retained)
- **Apple/Google**: For app store distribution and billing

## Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

- **Access**: View all data stored in Profile → Data Export (Elite tier)
- **Deletion**: Clear all data in Profile → Delete Account
- **Correction**: Update profile information anytime
- **Portability**: Export data as JSON (Elite tier)
- **Opt-out**: Disable analytics in Profile settings
- **Withdraw Consent**: Delete app to remove all local data

## Children's Privacy

Starz Cosmic Oracle is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us to delete it.

## Security

- All data stored locally on your device
- No passwords or sensitive financial data stored
- Subscription processing handled by Apple/Google secure systems
- AI processing via encrypted HTTPS to HuggingFace

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted in-app and on our website. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

## Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests:
- In-app: Profile → Support
- Email: privacy@starzcosmicoracle.com
